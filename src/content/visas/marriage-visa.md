The Thai marriage visa is tailored for foreign nationals who have tied the knot with Thai citizens, offering an opportunity to build a life together in this beautiful country. This visa grants you the right to reside in Thailand for a year, with the option of annual renewal provided you meet the stipulated requirements. Moreover, it opens doors to various privileges, including property ownership, bank account establishment, vehicle registration, and even employment opportunities within Thailand.

To secure a marriage visa, it’s essential to ensure that both you and your partner meet the following criteria:

You must be legally married to a Thai citizen.

Financial eligibility is crucial, whether through maintaining THB 400,000 in a Thai bank account for two months prior to application or demonstrating a monthly income of at least THB 40,000.

During your stay, regular reporting to the immigration office every 90 days is mandatory if your stay exceeds this duration. For those with multiple-entry visas, the flexibility to exit and re-enter at any time is available. However, holders of single-entry visas or those with extensions must obtain a re-entry permit before leaving Thailand.

Additionally, the marriage visa permits you to pursue employment opportunities in Thailand. Throughout the application process, you may be required to furnish an updated bank statement and a letter confirming the deposit of funds from an overseas source for at least two months.
