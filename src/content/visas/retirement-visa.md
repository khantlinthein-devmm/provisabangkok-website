## Non-Immigrant (Non-O) Retirement Visas

**Non-Immigrant Retirement Visa O**: This visa allows you to stay in Thailand for an extended period with renewals annually

**Non-Immigrant Retirement Visa O-A**: This visa is ideal for those wanting a longer stay with less than a decade long commitment

**Non-Immigrant Retirement Visa O-X (Long Stay 10 Years)**: This visa is perfect for those who desire a more permanent residency in Thailand

## Where to Apply for Each Visa Type

For Non-Immigrant O Visas, apply within Thailand

For Non-Immigrant O-A and O-X Visas, apply at the Royal Thai Embassy/Consulate in your home country

## Eligibility for All Visas

- Age 50+
- Clear record for entry to Thailand
- No criminal history in Thailand or home country
- Application from the same country
- Free from specific diseases
- Not allowed to work in Thailand

## Basic Requirements for All Visas

- Valid passport (with at least six months of validity and two blank pages)
- Completed visa forms, fee, and photos
- Evidence of family relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificate) if applicable

## Health insurance

**Non-Immigrant Visa O** visa applicants do not have to carry insurance coverage currently, but they are strongly encouraged to maintain valid insurance for medical reasons throughout their entire stay.

**Non-Immigrant Visa O-A** visa applicants must carry insurance coverage for the duration of their stay for Outpatient and Inpatient Treatment of no less than 3,000,000 baht. or 100,000 USD.

**Non-Immigrant Visa O-X** visa applicants must carry insurance coverage for the duration of their stay for outpatient treatment of no less than 40,000 baht and inpatient treatment of no less than 400,000 baht.

## For O-A and O-X Visas

A medical certificate is required declaring that you are free from specific diseases, Leprosy, Tuberculosis, drug addiction, Elephantiasis and third phase of Syphilis.

O-X visas are limited to passport holders from specific countries: Japan, Australia, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, Canada, and United States

## Financial Requirements for the O and O-A Visas

Certified bank statement showing a deposit of at least 800,000 Baht or proof of monthly income of 65,000 Baht held in a bank for no less than twelve months (contact us to learn more)

Letter of guarantee from the Thai bank if submitting bank statement

## Financial Requirements for the O-X Visa

Must have a bank deposit of no less than 3 million baht in Thailand or a bank deposit of no less than 1.8 million baht and annual income of no less than 1.2 million baht.

## Important Note on financial requirements

All bank documents must be certified, and maintenance of amounts, source, etc. are dependent on everyone.

Immigration Officers have varied discretion to verify income sources as well as request additional verification, and deny applications without offering a reason.

Thailand bank requirements for foreigners vary.

Please contact us to learn more.

## Health Insurance Requirements

The O Visa does not currently require health insurance coverage

Non-Immigrant Visa O-A visa applicants must carry insurance coverage for the duration of their stay for Outpatient and Inpatient Treatment of no less than 3,000,000 baht or 100,000 USD and Non-Immigrant Visa O-X visa applicants must carry insurance coverage for the duration of their stay for outpatient treatment of no less than 40,000 baht and inpatient treatment of no less than 400,000 baht

## Additional Considerations

- Thailand immigration procedures and requirements may change
- Ensure all documents are accurate and complete to avoid delays
- Visa processing fees are non-refundable
