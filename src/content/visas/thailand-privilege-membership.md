Experience Thailand like never before with the Thailand Privilege Membership Program. Formerly known as the Thailand Elite Visa, this exclusive program offers individuals the opportunity to experience the richness of Thai culture and lifestyle like never before for up to 20 years.

**Gold (5 years):** For ฿900,000, enjoy 20 privilege points and exclusive benefits such as airport welcome and member support.

**Platinum (10 years):** Elevate your experience with a 10-year membership for ฿1.5 million. Receive 35 privilege points, a special birthday gift, and all Gold-tier benefits.

## How to Apply

Gather the following documents:

- Completed Application Form (filled and signed)
- Copy of your passport’s identification page (passport validity must exceed 1 year from membership registration)
- High-resolution color ID photo
- PDPA form (proof of consent)
- Other documents if applicable (e.g., marriage certificate for family applications, Thailand arrival documents for residents)
- Copy of proof of relationship for Immediate Family of Main Member (if applying as a family, for applicants 19 years old or younger)

Simply fill out our Online Application form and upload the required documents. Once received, our team will manage the rest, including submission for background checks at the Thai Government Office. The approval process typically takes between 30 to 90 days, depending on nationality.

The THB 50,000 application fee is included in the Membership Fee and is refundable only if the background check fails.

## Payment

Upon approval, Thailand Privilege will provide bank account details for payment. Payment must be made directly to Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd through one of the following methods:

- Wire transfer from overseas or local bank accounts
- Online payment via credit card through the provided link

Approval typically takes 6 to 10 weeks. You will receive an email with further instructions upon approval.

## Important

- Not eligible for those with a volunteer visa
- Education visa applicants need other documents (school license, registration, certification, etc.), with approval not guaranteed
