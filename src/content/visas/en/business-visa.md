**Non-Immigrant “B” Business Visa – Visas for Working or Doing Business in Thailand**

The Thailand Non-Immigrant “B” Business Visa offers an attractive prospect for individuals seeking an extended stay in Thailand for business purposes, granting them the privilege to remain in the country for up to 15 months initially, with the added advantage of annual renewal options.

## Here are the basics:

There are different Non-Immigrant Visa categories depending on your specific business needs. Some common ones include:

- Business Visa (Category “B”) – This is a general visa for business activities
- Business-Approved Visa (Category “B-A”) – This visa is for specific business activities pre-approved by the Thai government
- Investment and Business Visa (Category “IB”) – This visa is for those who will be investing in a Thai business

A work permit is always required.

## Requirements for Business Visa (Category B)

- Valid passport for at least 6 months beyond your intended stay, with at least two blank pages
- Completed Visa Application Form
- Passport-sized Photo
- Proof of Financial Support

**Additional Documents (Depending on Your Activity)**

**For Working in Thailand (Requires Employer Sponsorship)**

- Letter of Approval from the Ministry of Labour (MOL) – Obtained by your Thai company through Form WP3
- Work permit issued by the MOL: Granted after a successful visa application

**For Conducting Business (Without Working)**

- Letter from your company explaining your position, length of employment, salary, and purpose of visit(s) to Thailand
- Proof of financial status (if self-employed), bank statements or financial documents demonstrating your ability to support yourself during your stay
- An invitation letter from a Thai business partner (optional) will strengthen your application by showing established business relationships in Thailand

## Company Registration in Thailand

Once you have your business visa, you can proceed with registering your company in Thailand. This process involves several steps:

- Choose a Business Structure – sole proprietorship, partnership, or limited company
- Reserve a unique name for your company with the Department of Business Development (DBD)
- Prepare Registration Documents, including company charter, shareholder information, memorandum of association (if applicable), and proof of registered office address
- Submit your application and required fees to the DBD
- Upon successful registration, you’ll receive a business certificate, officially registering your company in Thailand

## Documents from Your Thai Company (if applicable)

These documents mirror those required from your employer, demonstrating the legitimacy and financial stability of your Thai business partner.

- Company registration documents – Business registration certificate and business license
- Company profile – Overview of the company’s operations, products, and services
- Financial statements – Balance sheet, income tax statement, and business tax statement for the latest year
- Value-added tax (VAT) registration – Proof of VAT registration (Por Por 20)
- List of foreign workers – Details of existing foreign employees in the company (names, nationalities, positions)
- Map showing company location – Helps verify the company’s physical presence
- Letter of invitation – Formal letter inviting you to work for the company in Thailand, outlining your position, purpose of visit, and expected duration of stay

## Important Reminders for Business Visa

- Visa processing fees apply and are non-refundable
- Processing time generally takes 5-10 business days, but can vary
- Regulations can change, so always confirm the latest requirements before applying
- Missing documents or incomplete applications can lead to delays or rejection
- Immigration officials have the authority to request additional documents and may refuse applications without explanation

## Work Permit Application (if working in Thailand)

**Documents Required**

- Completed work permit application form.
- Passport copy with valid visa
- Company registration documents (certificate, business license)
- Letter of Approval from the Ministry of Labour (MOL) obtained by your company using Form WP3
- Educational certificates and relevant work experience documents
- Police clearance certificate from your home country (in some cases)
- Medical certificate from a certified hospital in Thailand

## Additional Considerations

Family Visas – If your family members will accompany you to Thailand, they can apply for separate visas (e.g., Non-Immigrant Visa Category “O”)
