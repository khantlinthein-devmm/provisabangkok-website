The SMART visa is available to highly skilled workers, investors, executives, or startup entrepreneurs who want to work and/or invest in one of the following industries:

- Next-Generation Automotive
- Smart Electronics
- Affluent, Medical, and Wellness Tourism
- Agriculture and Biotechnology
- Food for the Future
- Automation and Robotics
- Biofuels and Biochemicals
- Digital
- Medical Hub
- Alternative Dispute Resolution
- Human Resource Development in Science and Technology
- Environmental Management and Renewable Energy

## Types of Thailand SMART Visas

1. **SMART Visa “T”** for Foreign Talents
2. **SMART Visa “I”** for Foreign Investors
3. **SMART Visa “E”** for Foreign Executives
4. **SMART Visa “S”** for Foreign technology-based Startup Entrepreneurs
5. **SMART Visa “O”** for the family members (dependents spouse, and children) of one of the main SMART Visa holders above
