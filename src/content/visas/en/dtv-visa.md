## What is the DTV?

The Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) is a 5-year, multiple-entry visa for remote workers, digital nomads, freelancers and people coming to Thailand for “soft power” activities such as Muay Thai, Thai cooking or medical treatment. Each entry allows a stay of up to 180 days, which can be extended once by another 180 days at an immigration office.

## Who can apply

**Workcation (remote work)**

- Remote employees of companies outside Thailand
- Freelancers and digital nomads whose clients are outside Thailand

**Thai soft power activities**

- Muay Thai training
- Thai cooking courses
- Medical treatment
- Seminars and other activities organised by recognised Thai institutions

**Family members**

- The spouse and children under 20 of a DTV holder

## Key conditions

- Main applicant must be at least 20 years old
- Visa valid for 5 years, with multiple entries
- Stay of up to 180 days per entry
- One extension of up to 180 days per entry at Thai Immigration (fee ฿1,900)
- Proof of at least ฿500,000 (or the equivalent) in savings
- Working for a Thai employer is not allowed; you may only work remotely for employers or clients outside Thailand
- 90-day address reporting still applies during long stays
- You must apply from outside Thailand, through the Thai e-Visa system

## Required documents

- Passport valid for at least 6 months
- Recent passport-size photo
- Bank statements showing at least ฿500,000 (or the equivalent), usually covering the last 6 months
- Proof of your current location, if your embassy asks for it

**For the workcation track**

- Employment contract or certificate from a company outside Thailand, or a portfolio and client contracts if you are a freelancer
- Employer letter confirming remote work and salary (if employed)

**For the soft power track**

- Acceptance or enrolment letter from a registered Thai institution (programme, duration, schedule and fee), or a medical appointment letter

**For family members**

- Marriage or birth certificate, and a copy of the main applicant’s DTV
- A further ฿500,000 in funds for each dependant

## Fees

- Visa fee: ฿10,000, charged in local currency (the exact amount varies by embassy)
- Extension of stay at Immigration: ฿1,900
