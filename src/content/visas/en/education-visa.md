The Thailand Non-Immigrant Education “ED” Visa offers a unique opportunity to pursue educational goals while staying in this captivating country.

One key advantage of the Thai ED Visa is that it allows you to stay in Thailand for the entire visa duration, eliminating the need for frequent “visa runs.” However, visa holders must report to the Royal Thai Immigration office every 90 days to confirm their address.

If you’re considering obtaining a One-Year Non-Immigrant Education “ED” Visa, our services are comprehensive. We also keep our clients well-informed about any changes in Thai Immigration regulations, ensuring a successful journey towards acquiring the Thai Education “ED” Visa.

We provide essential consultation and guidance throughout the process, ensuring a smooth application and compliance with Thai Immigration requirements.

## To Study at a Higher Education Level - (University and College)

- Passport or travel document with a validity of not less than 6 months with at least two blank pages
- Photograph of the applicant, taken within the past six months
- Letter confirming enrolment details from the school signed by an authorized person together with a copy of the ID card or passport of the signer
- University’s registration certificate.
- Academic certificate from previous education level

## Internship (Curricular internship), Attending Workshop Training or Seminar Organized by International Organization

- Passport or travel document with a validity of not less than 6 months with at least two blank pages
- Photograph of the applicant, taken within the past six months
- Letter confirming the internship/participation in a program from the company/international organization
- Letter confirming the curricular internship from the visa applicant’s university/educational institute

## Buddhism Studies (Monk) or Performing Buddhism Ritual Activities

- Passport or travel document with a validity of not less than 6 months with at least two blank pages
- Photograph of the applicant, taken within the past six months
- Approval letter from the National office of Buddhism
- Confirmation letter from the host temple
- Academic certificate from the previous level

## Foreigner wishes to perform Buddhism ritual activities

- Passport or travel document with a validity of not less than 6 months with at least two blank pages
- Photograph of the applicant, taken within the past six months
- Travel booking confirmation
- Financial evidence, e.g., bank statements, proof of earnings, proof of sponsorship from a third party
- Confirmation letter from the host temple

## Foreigners Seeking to Promote or Involve in a Religious Mission with Approval from the Thai Concerned authority

- Passport or travel document with a validity of not less than 6 months with at least two blank pages
- Photograph of the applicant, taken within the past six months
- Approval letter from the concerned Thai authority
- Confirmation letter from the host religious organization
- Religious organization’s registration certificate
