Planning to work in Thailand? Here’s your essential guide to securing a work permit.

**Why You Need a Work Permit:** A work permit is a legal document that outlines your job description, position, and employer. It’s mandatory for all foreigners wishing to work in Thailand, regardless of visa type.

**Step 1: Obtain a Non-Immigrant Visa** Before you can apply for a work permit, you need a non-immigrant visa. This visa must be secured before entering Thailand.

**Step 2: Apply for the Work Permit** With your visa in hand, you can start the work permit application process. This typically takes about 7 business days and is handled by the Ministry of Labor.

**Eligibility Criteria:**

- You must have a non-immigrant or resident visa.
- Your employer must be ready to provide the necessary documents.
- Your job must not be on the prohibited list for foreigners.

**Required Documents:**

**For Applicants:**

- 2-inch photo
- Medical certificate
- Original passport
- Letter of employment
- Degree certificate
- Thai address

**For Employers:**

- Company certificate and objectives
- Shareholders list
- VAT application
- Company’s withholding tax records
- Financial statements
- Director’s passport copy with signature
- Office map
- Employment letter stating your position and salary
- Employment agreement

**After Approval:** Always carry your work permit, especially at your workplace. Note that you can only perform the job specified in your permit for the listed employer.

This streamlined approach ensures you comply with Thai regulations, making your work experience in Thailand legal and hassle-free.
