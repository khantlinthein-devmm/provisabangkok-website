Congratulations on your marriage! Now that you’ve tied the knot with your Thai partner, it’s time to apply for a Thai Marriage Visa. This long-stay visa allows you to live with your spouse in Thailand and paves the way for potential permanent residency.

**Benefits of a Thai Marriage Visa:**

- **Extended Stay:** You get a 1-year stay, renewable annually.
- **Ease of Travel:** No need for a re-entry permit when traveling in and out of Thailand.
- **Work Opportunities:** You can apply for a work permit and work for a Thai company, simplifying your job search.

**Requirements for a Thai Marriage Visa:**

- **Marriage to a Thai National:** You must be legally married to a Thai citizen.
- **Marriage Documentation:** Provide a Marriage Certificate (Kor Ror 3) or Marriage Registration (Kor Ror 2). If married abroad, you’ll need a Kor Ror 22 (Registration of Family Status).
- **Proof of Residency:** Show proof of your or your spouse’s address in Thailand.
- **Financial Requirements:** Maintain a deposit of at least 400,000 THB in a Thai bank account for 2 months or have a monthly income of 40,000 THB. Provide an updated bankbook, a bank letter, or an income affidavit from your embassy.
- **Supplementary Documentation:** Some countries require an Affirmation of Freedom to Marry document and translated documents verified by a consulate notary or embassy.

**Application Process:**

1. **Obtain a 90-Day Non-Immigrant Visa:** Apply at the nearest Royal Thai Consulate or Embassy.
2. **Gather Required Documents:** Collect all necessary paperwork to convert your 90-day visa to a 1-year marriage visa.
3. **Submit Application:** When 30 days remain on your non-immigrant visa, submit all documents and a visa extension request to the Thai Immigration Bureau.

**Conditions to Fulfill:**

- **90-Day Reporting:** Report your current residence to Thai immigration every 90 days.
- **Re-Entry Permit:** Obtain a single-entry or multiple-entry permit before leaving Thailand.
- **Funds Maintenance:** Have at least 3 months’ worth of funds in your account before renewing the visa.
- **Post-Divorce Visa Status:** If divorced, you must forfeit the visa and leave Thailand, although some exceptions allow you to stay until the visa expires.
