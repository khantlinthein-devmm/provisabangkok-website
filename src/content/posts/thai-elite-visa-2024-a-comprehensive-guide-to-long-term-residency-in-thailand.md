**Eligibility and Requirements**

To be eligible for the Thai Elite Visa, applicants must meet specific criteria and provide necessary documentation:

- **Financial Stability:** Proof of financial capability to cover the cost of the visa and living expenses.
- **Documentation:** Completed application form, copy of passport, recent photograph, and financial statements.

**Application Process**

1. **Submit Application:** Complete and submit the application form along with the required documents.
2. **Pay Membership Fee:** Pay the applicable membership fee for the chosen visa package.
3. **Approval Process:** Wait for approval, which typically takes around one month.
4. **Complete Visa Formalities:** Once approved, complete the visa application process at a Thai Embassy or Consulate.
