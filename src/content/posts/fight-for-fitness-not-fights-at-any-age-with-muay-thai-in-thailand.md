While Muay Thai gyms can be found worldwide, but there’s something truly special about training in its birthplace, Thailand. With various visa options your Muay Thai fitness adventure is closer than ever!

Many wonder if Muay Thai training is age specific. No, but it is body specific. Let’s face it, not everyone can walk into a Muay Thai gym and learn to be a fighter, but that’s just one facet of the sport.

Looking to lose weight, gain muscle tone, learn self-defense techniques, and meet amazing people in an incredible location? Thailand is the perfect destination.

**Fitness Transformation in One Year**

What will one year of training in Muay Thai give someone who doesn’t want to be fighter, but wants to learn Muay Thai and be in good fitness? One year of Muay Thai training in Thailand will transform your life, regardless of age or experience. You’ll return home with a newfound confidence and a physique that will leave everyone impressed.

Leave worries at home, and look at Muay Thai as an incredible fitness journey, a gateway to a healthier lifestyle and if you don’t want to fight, simply forget the fight ring.

Remember, you’re in charge of your experience. Muay Thai is a great fitness journey of self-discovery, not necessarily a fight camp. Communication with your trainer is key. Let them know your desire for a fitness-focused, non-competitive experience.

So, what does that year look like?

**Three Phases to Fitness Success**

In phase one, typically three months, you will master the basics – punches, kicks, and blocks – with proper form. Build endurance and core strength. In phase two, or months 4 to 6, you will refine your technique, add knee and elbow strikes, and practice controlled sparring drills for defense. By then your self-confidence and general fitness will be at an all time high. The last phase is intense, but you will be ready. Here you will take your pad work and bag training to another level. You will also learn advanced footwork and counters. The footwork will pay big benefits as you age. All the while you’ll be developing a deep appreciation for Thai culture through gym traditions.

Muay Thai training will cultivate a sense of discipline, perseverance and confidence in anyone who wants it. And who knows, you just might find your soulmate!

And then what? Well. As they say in Thailand, “It’s up to you?”
