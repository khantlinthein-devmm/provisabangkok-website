## Thai Elite Visa

The Thailand Elite Visa is a long-term visa under the Privilege Entry Visa category. It offers residency in Thailand for periods ranging from 5 to 20 years, depending on the chosen package. The Elite Visa is a 5-year renewable multiple-entry visa with a 1-year stay per entry.

This visa is ideal for digital nomads, retirees, and businesspeople who frequently visit Thailand and wish to avoid the hassle of Thailand’s visa system.

## Tourist Visa

The Tourist Visa to Thailand allows for a 60-day stay and can be either multiple-entry or single-entry. It can be extended for an additional 30 days at an immigration office. For longer stays, you can convert the Tourist Visa to a Non-Immigrant Visa.

## Non-Immigrant Visa

The Non-Immigrant Visa is typically a single-entry visa valid for 90 days. Depending on the visa type, it can also allow you to get a work permit and open a bank account. This visa can be extended to a long-term visa. Types include:

- **Non-B Business Visa**
- **Non-O Retirement Visa**
- **Non-O Marriage Visa**
- **Non-B Investment Visa**
- **Long-Term Residence Visa**

## Marriage Visa and Retirement Visa

There is no direct application for Marriage and Retirement Visas. To qualify, you must first obtain a Non-Immigrant Visa and then convert it. For a Marriage Visa, you need to be married to a Thai national. For a Retirement Visa, you must be 50 years or older. Both visas allow a 1-year stay, renewable without leaving Thailand.

## Business Visa

The Non-Immigrant Business Visa is designed for those who want to work or do business in Thailand. With this visa, you can also obtain a work permit and open a bank account. It can be extended annually as long as employment or business continues.

## Permanent Resident Visa

To qualify for a Permanent Resident Visa, you must have stayed in Thailand for 3 consecutive years with 1-year visa extensions. If married to a Thai citizen, you must earn 40,000 THB per month. If single, your monthly income must be at least 80,000 THB. You need to present 3 years of personal tax statements to prove your income.
