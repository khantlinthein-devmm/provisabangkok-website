Thailand remains one of the world's most attractive retirement havens, offering exceptional healthcare standards, affordable living costs, and warm tropical weather. However, renewing your 1-Year Retirement Extension of Stay (Non-Immigrant O or Non-Immigrant OA) requires strict compliance with Royal Thai Immigration financial seasoning rules and documentation deadlines.

This comprehensive guide details everything retirees need to know about the 800,000 THB bank deposit seasoning timeframe, alternative income verification, health insurance mandates, and immigration office procedures in Bangkok.

## Retirement Visa Categories: Non-O vs Non-OA

Before planning your annual renewal, it is essential to distinguish between the two primary retirement visa streams:

1. **Non-Immigrant O (In-Country Retirement Extension):** Issued inside Thailand based on retirement. It does not carry mandatory commercial health insurance requirements under standard Immigration Division 1 procedures.
2. **Non-Immigrant OA (Long Stay Applied from Abroad):** Issued by Thai Royal Embassies or Consulates in your home country. Annual renewals of Non-OA visas require continuous approved health insurance coverage.

## Financial Requirements and the 800,000 THB Seasoning Rule

Thai immigration law mandates that applicants aged 50 years or older satisfy one of three financial criteria:

### Option 1: 800,000 THB Fixed Bank Deposit
You must maintain a minimum balance of 800,000 Thai Baht deposited in a personal savings or fixed-deposit account at a licensed commercial bank in Thailand (e.g., Bangkok Bank, Kasikornbank, SCB).

- **Seasoning Before Renewal:** The full 800,000 THB balance must be maintained in the account for at least 2 full months (60 days) prior to the day of your renewal application (or 3 months for first renewals in certain jurisdictions).
- **Post-Extension Maintenance:** Following approval, the balance must remain at 800,000 THB or higher for at least 3 months.
- **Minimum Floor Balance:** For the remainder of the visa year, the balance may never drop below 400,000 THB.

### Option 2: 65,000 THB Monthly Income Method
Applicants receiving a monthly pension, social security, or regular overseas dividend income can verify a regular inflow of at least 65,000 THB per month.

- **Transfer Proof:** The income must be transferred into a Thai bank account from abroad via International Funds Transfer (FET / SWIFT FTT code) showing the international remitter.
- **Statement Period:** You must present at least 12 consecutive months of bank statements demonstrating foreign monthly remittances of 65,000 THB or more.

### Option 3: Combination Method
A combined total of annual pension income plus bank deposit equaling at least 800,000 THB over a 12-month period.

## Required Document Checklist for Retirement Extension

When attending your renewal appointment at Chaengwattana Immigration Division 1 (or your local provincial immigration office), bring the following documents:

### Personal Identification:
- Original passport valid for at least 6 months, plus photocopies of photo page, original Non-Immigrant visa, all extension stamps, re-entry permits, and current TM.6 arrival slip.
- Two recent passport-sized photographs (4x6 cm, blue or white background, taken within 6 months).
- Completed and signed Application for Extension of Temporary Stay (Form TM.7).
- Extension fee: 1,900 THB in exact cash.

### Banking Documentation (Dated on Day of Submission or Day Prior):
- Original updated Thai bank passbook showing transactions up to the appointment date.
- Formal Bank Confirmation Letter issued by the bank branch verifying account ownership and current balance (dated within 1 to 7 days of application).
- Certified 12-month bank statement stamped by bank branch officials.
- Foreign Exchange Transaction (FET) credit advices if using the monthly income transfer method.

### Residential Verification:
- Copy of lease agreement or title deed (Chanote) for your Thai residence.
- Official TM.30 Receipt of Notification of Residence issued to your landlord or property owner.
- Hand-drawn map to your residence from the nearest major landmark or BTS/MRT station.

## Step-by-Step Renewal Process

### Step 1: Pre-Audit Financials (60 to 90 Days Before Expiry)
Ensure your 800,000 THB bank deposit has crossed the mandatory 60-day seasoning threshold with zero withdrawals dipping beneath the target limit.

### Step 2: Bank Certification (24 to 48 Hours Before Application)
Visit your Thai bank branch. Update your passbook, make a nominal deposit or withdrawal (e.g., 100 THB) to ensure the current day's date is printed in the book, and request the official Immigration Bank Letter and 12-month statement.

### Step 3: Immigration Submission (Up to 30 to 45 Days in Advance)
You do not have to wait until the final expiration day. Most immigration offices in Thailand permit renewals up to 30 days (and up to 45 days in Bangkok) before your current permission of stay expires. Submitting early protects you against last-minute administrative delays.

### Step 4: Stamping and Re-Entry Permit
Upon review and interview, the immigration officer places the 1-year extension stamp into your passport. If you plan to travel outside Thailand during the upcoming year, proceed immediately to the Re-Entry Permit counter to secure a Single (1,000 THB) or Multiple (3,800 THB) Re-Entry Permit.

## Crucial Ongoing Responsibilities for Retirees

1. **90-Day Reporting (TM.47):** Every 90 consecutive days spent in Thailand requires filing a report of address with immigration. This can be completed online via the Immigration Bureau portal, by registered postal mail, or through an authorized visa agent.
2. **Maintaining Address Currency (TM.30):** Whenever you move to a new condo or return to Thailand from an international flight, a new TM.30 filing is required within 24 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I touch the 800,000 THB deposit after my visa extension is approved?
Only partially, and strictly after 3 months have elapsed from the renewal stamp date. For the subsequent months, your balance must never drop below 400,000 THB until the two months preceding your next annual renewal, when it must be topped back up to 800,000 THB.

### What happens if my bank balance accidentally drops below 800,000 THB during the 2-month seasoning window?
Immigration officers scrutinize daily statement ledgers strictly. If the balance falls beneath 800,000 THB for even a single day during the 60-day window, the application will be denied. Contact a professional visa agency immediately to review legal remedy options, such as an emergency under-consideration period or in-country status reset.

### Is health insurance required for standard Non-Immigrant O retirement extensions?
Under current Royal Thai Immigration regulations, standard Non-Immigrant O extensions processed in Thailand do not mandate commercial insurance. In contrast, Non-Immigrant OA visas require certified policies offering at least 3,000,000 THB (or 100,000 USD) medical coverage.
